# Code Of Conduct

This project expects professional behavior from everyone participating in it.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate directly and respectfully.
- Assume good intent, then resolve disagreements with evidence.
- Give feedback on code and decisions, not on people.
- Make room for different levels of experience and different working styles.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory behavior.
- Personal attacks, insults, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Disruptive conduct that blocks collaboration.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this standard. Repeated or severe violations may lead to restricted access to project spaces.

## Reporting

Report serious concerns through a private maintainer channel rather than a public issue. Replace this guidance with a dedicated contact route once one exists.
